Our Story

VeeMoo Bites is a Bloemfontein-based business specialising in biltong and curated meat hampers, delivered straight to the customer's door. Started to bring quality, convenient biltong to time-strapped students and young professionals, VeeMoo Bites has grown through word of mouth and social media, becoming known for its popular Student Hampers.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality, affordable meat hampers straight to the customer's door, making good biltong accessible without the hassle.

Our Vision

To become Bloemfontein's go-to name for meat hampers among students and young professionals.

Why Choose Us

Fast delivery across Bloemfontein.

Quality biltong and meat products.

Student-friendly prices.
